# Grant and program reporting

## Inputs

- Signed grant letter/agreement, amendments, award and payment schedule, allowed uses, periods, conditions, reporting requirements, matching requirements and grant contact.
- Ledger revenue/expense detail plus classes/projects, allocation policy and approved budget.
- Ramp invoice/payment detail for open commitments not yet in ledger and bank/receipts as relevant.

## Review logic

1. Create a grant-by-grant register with unique grant ID, donor, restriction type, covered programs, start/end dates, award amount, cash received, income recognition policy, spending rules, reporting dates and supporting reference.
2. Identify donor restrictions separately from board-designated or management budget buckets. Clarify unconditional vs conditional grants and revenue-recognition treatment with the accountant; do not treat receipt date as automatic revenue date.
3. Compute separate metrics: `awarded`, `cash received`, `revenue recognized`, `eligible expense incurred`, `cash paid`, `committed but unpaid`, `remaining award authorization`, and `restricted net assets` where supported. State the definition used for each.
4. Match actual spending to supported grant ID + accounting period + natural account. Expenses with missing allocation or mixed use go into an exception queue, not an arbitrary grant.
5. Report actual versus budget by program and by grant separately; reconcile totals to the ledger and explain timing differences with Ramp and statements.
6. Escalate grant restrictions, potentially unallowable expenses, reporting uncertainty, and negative balances for review.

## Report example (illustrative, NOT actual amounts)

| Grant | Award | Cash received | Eligible expenses | Open commitments | Remaining authorization | Exceptions |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Grant A | $100,000 | $60,000 | $40,000 | $10,000 | $50,000 | One disputed allocation |

Remaining authorization above equals award minus eligible expenses minus commitments; it is neither cash balance nor restricted net assets. Adapt formula to the agreement and accounting policy.
