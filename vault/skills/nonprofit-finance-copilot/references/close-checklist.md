# Monthly close checklist

Use the organization's actual period and accounting basis. Every step needs evidence and a named reviewer.

1. **Scope and completeness**: Confirm account list, bank/credit-card statements, Ramp exports, ledger detail, legacy payroll provider balances if relevant, fiscal month, and posting cutoff.
2. **Vendor/AP**: Check open Ramp bills, paid invoices, credits, duplicate bills, payment reversals and attachments. Match one source invoice to one ledger payable/expense and settlement. Review bills paid outside Ramp.
3. **Cash and liabilities**: Compare each month-end bank statement to the ledger and reconciliation report; list unmatched deposits/withdrawals, outstanding payments, transfers, processor timing differences, and stale unreconciled items. Do not book a plug entry to eliminate differences.
4. **Classification**: Review uncategorized records, fixed assets, prepaids, accruals (if accrual basis), allocation splits, grant/project tags, and restricted-fund entries. Do not invent allocations; apply written policies.
5. **Income and grants**: Match donor/grant deposit evidence, check restrictions, release policies, receivable status, designated-vs-donor-restricted distinctions, and deferred/conditional grants as applicable.
6. **Statements**: Prepare Statement of Activities (by program and natural classification if available), Statement of Financial Position, cash movement, budget-vs-actual, and grant roll-forward. Verify balance sheet equations and accounting period.
7. **Review packet**: Publish summary, exceptions, aging, proposed journal entries (not posted), source record references, material changes from previous month, and approval requests.
8. **Close sign-off**: Ask authorized bookkeeper or CPA to confirm reconciliations and proposed entries; let only an authorized human lock the period. Record who approved and when, but don't fabricate this confirmation.

Suggested exception severity: Critical = potential duplicate payment, missing cash, restricted-fund misuse or unexplained material variance; High = unreconciled statement or missing grant agreement; Medium = missing document/category; Low = minor metadata issue. Thresholds and materiality must be set by the organization.
